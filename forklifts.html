Types of Forklifts

Electric Forklifts

Zero-emission, quiet and ideal for indoor warehousing, cold stores and food-grade sites. Available with lead-acid or lithium batteries from 1.0t up to heavy-duty capacities.

Diesel Forklifts

Powerful and durable for outdoor yards, construction and rough surfaces — from 1.5t general handling to 10t+ heavy industrial and container work.

LPG & Petrol Forklifts

Flexible indoor/outdoor forklifts with fast refuelling — popular for warehouses, loading docks and general handling.

Reach Trucks & Narrow-Aisle

High-lift electric trucks for warehouse racking up to 12m+, maximising storage in narrow aisles.

What Size Forklift Do I Need?

Start with your heaviest load and the height you need to lift it. Remember a forklift’s rated capacity is at a standard load centre and at ground level — real capacity drops as you lift higher and as the load sits further from the mast. NOYO shows capacity at height for every forklift so you match the machine to the actual lift, not just the badge rating. Factor in indoor vs outdoor use (tyres and emissions), aisle width, and whether you need attachments like slippers, jibs or container masts.

Forklifts — Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right forklift capacity?

Base it on your heaviest load plus a safety margin, and check capacity at your actual lift height and load centre — not just the rated figure. NOYO shows capacity-at-height for every forklift so you spec correctly.

Electric, diesel or LPG forklift — which is best?

Electric suits indoor and emission-sensitive work; diesel suits outdoor yards and rough ground; LPG is a flexible indoor/outdoor all-rounder. NOYO lets you filter by power source.

Can NOYO help with forklift hire and purchase?

Yes — NOYO matches you to the right forklift for hire or sale and connects you to enquire, anywhere in Australia.

What is a reach truck?

A reach truck is a narrow-aisle electric forklift that lifts to 12m or more for high-bay warehouse racking, maximising storage density.
