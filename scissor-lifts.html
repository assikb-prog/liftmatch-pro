Choose the Right Scissor Lift for the Job

Scissor lifts give you a stable platform straight up for working at height — perfect for maintenance, fit-out, construction and warehousing. The right choice comes down to working height, platform capacity, deck size and whether you’re on smooth indoor slabs or rough outdoor ground. NOYO compares electric slab scissor lifts (quiet, zero-emission, narrow enough for doorways and lifts) against diesel rough-terrain scissor lifts (4WD, high capacity, built for site work) so you get the right access equipment for your reach and surface.

Types of Scissor Lifts

Electric Scissor Lifts

Zero-emission, non-marking tyres and compact widths for indoor work — retail, warehousing, facilities and fit-out. Platform heights from around 4m to 15m+.

Rough-Terrain Scissor Lifts

Diesel 4WD machines with outriggers and high deck capacities for construction and outdoor sites on uneven ground. Working heights to 18m+.

Narrow & Low-Level

Ultra-narrow models that fit through standard doorways and into lifts for tight indoor access.

What Height Scissor Lift Do I Need?

Work from the height you need to reach, then add the platform height that gets you there safely (a person adds roughly 2m of reach above the platform). Check the deck capacity for your workers plus tools and materials, and the machine width against your doorways and aisles. Indoor jobs on smooth floors suit electric slab scissors; outdoor jobs on rough or sloping ground need a diesel rough-terrain model. NOYO filters all of this for you.

Scissor Lifts — Frequently Asked Questions

What height scissor lift do I need?

Take your required working height and choose a platform height that reaches it safely — a worker adds about 2m above the deck. NOYO matches working height to the right model.

Can I use an electric scissor lift outdoors?

Electric slab scissors are designed for firm, level surfaces and light outdoor use. For rough, uneven or sloping ground you need a diesel 4WD rough-terrain scissor lift. NOYO flags which is which.

What is the difference between a scissor lift and a boom lift?

A scissor lift goes straight up for height. A boom lift reaches up and out, over and around obstacles. NOYO helps you compare both.
