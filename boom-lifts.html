Reach Up, Out and Over — Choose the Right Boom Lift

Boom lifts — also called cherry pickers or EWPs — give you height and horizontal reach to get over and around obstacles that a scissor lift can’t. The two main types are articulating (knuckle) booms for up-and-over access in tight spots, and telescopic (stick) booms for maximum height and straight-line outreach. NOYO matches you by working height, horizontal outreach, power source and terrain — from compact electric booms for indoor work to big diesel 4WD booms for construction sites.

Types of Boom Lifts

Articulating (Knuckle) Boom Lifts

Multiple pivoting sections reach up, out and over obstacles — ideal for steelwork, facades and congested sites. Electric and hybrid models work indoors and out.

Telescopic (Stick) Boom Lifts

A straight extending boom for the greatest height and outreach — built for open construction sites and high-level access to 28m+.

Electric & Hybrid Booms

Zero-emission and hybrid booms for emission-sensitive and indoor/outdoor work without sacrificing reach.

Boom Lift vs Cherry Picker vs EWP — and What Size to Choose

"Cherry picker" and "EWP" (elevated work platform) are everyday names for boom lifts. To choose one, work from your working height and, crucially, your horizontal outreach — how far out from the base you need to reach — plus the up-and-over clearance if an obstacle sits between you and the work. Then match power (electric/hybrid for indoors and clean sites, diesel 4WD for rough terrain) and platform capacity. NOYO compares all of this in one search.

Boom Lifts — Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between an articulating and telescopic boom lift?

An articulating (knuckle) boom bends at multiple points to reach up and over obstacles in tight spaces. A telescopic (stick) boom extends in a straight line for maximum height and outreach on open sites.

Is a cherry picker the same as a boom lift?

Yes — "cherry picker" and "EWP" are common names for a boom lift. NOYO compares all types in one place.

How do I choose the right boom lift size?

Match both the working height and the horizontal outreach you need, plus up-and-over clearance for any obstacles. NOYO shows all three so you pick the right envelope.
