Choose the Right Telehandler for Lift, Reach and Capacity

Telehandlers combine the lifting power of a forklift with the reach of a crane — placing loads high and far forward on construction sites, farms and industrial yards. The right choice depends on maximum lift height, forward reach, and capacity at the working point (capacity drops as you extend out and up). NOYO compares fixed telehandlers for straight lift-and-place work against rotating (360°) telehandlers that slew the load without repositioning the machine.

Types of Telehandlers

Fixed Telehandlers

The workhorse of construction and agriculture — lift, reach and place loads from compact 4m machines to 17m+ high-reach models.

Rotating Telehandlers

A 360° slewing turret places loads anywhere around the machine without repositioning — ideal for congested sites and crane-style work with outriggers.

Compact Telehandlers

Low-height, narrow models for tight sites, sheds and indoor work.

Fixed vs Rotating Telehandler — What Do You Need?

If you’re lifting and placing loads in front of the machine, a fixed telehandler is efficient and cost-effective. If you need to place loads around the machine on a congested site — or want crane-like coverage from one set-up — a rotating telehandler with outriggers and 360° slew is the tool. Always check capacity at your working height and reach, not just the maximum rating, because it falls as the boom extends. NOYO shows capacity at the working point for every telehandler.

Telehandlers — Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a fixed and rotating telehandler?

A fixed telehandler lifts and places loads in front of the machine. A rotating telehandler has a 360° slewing turret to place loads all around it without repositioning — ideal for congested sites.

How much can a telehandler lift at height?

Capacity falls as the boom extends up and out, so always check capacity at your actual working point, not the maximum rating. NOYO shows capacity at the working point for every model.

Is a telehandler a type of forklift?

A telehandler is a telescopic-boom handler that lifts higher and reaches further forward than a standard forklift, combining forklift and crane-like abilities.
