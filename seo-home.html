What's the difference between a boom lift and a scissor lift?

A scissor lift raises a platform straight up for height only. A boom lift — also called a cherry picker or EWP — has an articulating or telescopic arm that reaches up, out and over obstacles. NOYO helps you compare both so you pick the right access equipment for the reach you need.
